## Validation Flow

```
Before Saving Shift
   ?
   ?? Is Shift Name empty?
   ?  ?? YES ? ?? "Shift Name is required."
   ?
   ?? Is Employee selected?
   ?  ?? NO ? ?? "Employee selection is required."
   ?
   ?? Is Department selected?
   ?  ?? NO ? ?? "Department selection is required."
   ?
   ?? Are times valid?
   ?  ?? NO ? ?? "Start/End Time must be valid."
   ?
   ?? Is Start Time < End Time?
   ?  ?? NO ? ?? "Start Time must be before End Time."
   ?
   ?? All Valid?
      ?? YES ? ? Proceed with Save
```
